# Statistics Mastery Check

Present a compact scenario and ask the learner what can be estimated, which assumptions matter, and how the result should be interpreted.

Include at least one tempting but invalid conclusion and ask the learner to identify why it fails.

## Teaching example

When teaching association, randomization, and causal claims, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for transfer and feedback. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
