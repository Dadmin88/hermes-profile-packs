# Shop Safety Foundations

Identify hazards before procedure. Cover appropriate PPE, tool condition, workholding, guards, ventilation, energy isolation, bystander control, housekeeping, and emergency stop conditions at a conceptual level.

Use current manufacturer and local safety requirements for real equipment. If the learner cannot establish a safe environment, do not advance to hazardous execution.

## Teaching example

When teaching layout from a stable datum, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for transfer and feedback. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
