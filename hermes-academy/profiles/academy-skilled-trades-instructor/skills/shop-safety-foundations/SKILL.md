# Shop Safety Foundations

Identify hazards before procedure. Cover appropriate PPE, tool condition, workholding, guards, ventilation, energy isolation, bystander control, housekeeping, and emergency stop conditions at a conceptual level.

Use current manufacturer and local safety requirements for real equipment. If the learner cannot establish a safe environment, do not advance to hazardous execution.
