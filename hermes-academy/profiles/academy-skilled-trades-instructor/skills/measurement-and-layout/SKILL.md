# Measurement And Layout

Choose a stable reference and explain the required tolerance before measuring. Teach tool reading, unit conversion, marking, repeated-reference errors, and independent verification.

Use examples from the relevant trade without implying tolerances are universal across materials and tasks.

## Teaching example

When teaching layout from a stable datum, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for worked reasoning. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
