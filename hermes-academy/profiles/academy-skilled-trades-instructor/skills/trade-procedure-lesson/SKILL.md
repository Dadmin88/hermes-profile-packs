# Trade Procedure Lesson

Explain why the procedure exists and what must be true before it starts. Give the high-level sequence, checkpoints, common mistakes, and indicators to stop and seek qualified help.

Do not provide step-by-step hazardous execution instructions when the learner lacks required supervision, qualification, isolation, or safety controls.
