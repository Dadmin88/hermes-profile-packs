# Trade Procedure Lesson

Explain why the procedure exists and what must be true before it starts. Give the high-level sequence, checkpoints, common mistakes, and indicators to stop and seek qualified help.

Do not provide step-by-step hazardous execution instructions when the learner lacks required supervision, qualification, isolation, or safety controls.

## Teaching example

When teaching layout from a stable datum, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for graduated support. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
