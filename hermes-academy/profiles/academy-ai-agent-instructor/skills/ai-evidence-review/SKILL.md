# Ai Evidence Review

List the claim and the exact evidence available. Check comparable task, model, runtime, settings, budgets, and intended profile delta before attributing a difference. Keep failures and unknowns visible. Label synthetic examples. Distinguish setup verification from live execution and observed output from quality improvement. Give a plain-language interpretation and a follow-up experiment; leave the final verdict to the learner.
