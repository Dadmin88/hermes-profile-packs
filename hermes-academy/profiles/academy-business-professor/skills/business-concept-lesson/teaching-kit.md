# Human teaching examples: Compute contribution and distinguish it from profit

These synthetic examples are optional scaffolding, not a required lesson script. Adapt to the learner's objective and level. They establish no real-world current facts, credentials, or professional recommendations.

## Diagnostic and misconception

A fictional product sells for 12 units of currency, variable cost is 7, monthly fixed cost 100. What is contribution per unit?

Watch for: Confusing revenue with contribution or contribution with total profit; mixing periods.

## Worked example

Contribution per unit is 12 minus 7 = 5. Twenty units cover fixed cost 100 in this simplified model. At that volume operating profit is zero, before any omitted costs. These are invented figures for numeracy.

## Hint ladder

Reveal one hint at a time only when needed; do not give all hints with a transfer task.

1. Separate fixed and variable costs.
2. What remains from one sale?
3. Apply that contribution to the same period fixed cost.

## Different transfer task

A fictional service charges 30 with variable cost 18 and fixed cost 240 per month. Compute contribution and simplified break-even volume; state an omitted assumption.

Do not expose the worked solution or feedback key before the learner attempts it, unless they explicitly request a direct answer.

## Feedback criteria

Credit 12 contribution and 20 units with matching periods; flag profit claims that ignore fixed or omitted costs.

Name the learner's correct reasoning first, then the smallest concrete correction. Ask for only the changed part if a retry is useful.

## Direct answer and demonstrated understanding

For a direct question: Revenue is value of sales; profit subtracts relevant costs, and cash received may occur in a different period.

When the learner already understands: I correctly calculated contribution and break-even; check only whether my interpretation confuses profit with cash. Assess the submitted reasoning; confirm correct work without adding obligatory practice. If no attempt is supplied, do not claim mastery.
