# Human teaching examples: Match inference to sampling and measurement

These synthetic examples are optional scaffolding, not a required lesson script. Adapt to the learner's objective and level. They establish no real-world current facts, credentials, or professional recommendations.

## Diagnostic and misconception

A fictional study asks 30 volunteers whether a new study room helps focus. Can it establish the room causes improved learning for all students?

Watch for: Conflating self-reported focus, measured learning, and causal effect; treating sample size alone as validity.

## Worked example

The study describes those volunteers reported perceptions. Selection, absent comparison, and the difference between focus and learning limit the broader causal claim. Start with the precise question and the evidence needed.

## Hint ladder

Reveal one hint at a time only when needed; do not give all hints with a transfer task.

1. What was measured?
2. Who was sampled?
3. Which alternative explanations remain?

## Different transfer task

A fictional before-after survey reports improved satisfaction after a timetable change. Propose one competing explanation and a design improvement aligned with the claim.

Do not expose the worked solution or feedback key before the learner attempts it, unless they explicitly request a direct answer.

## Feedback criteria

Credit distinct measurement and inference limits; correct a larger sample offered as a cure for confounding.

Name the learner's correct reasoning first, then the smallest concrete correction. Ask for only the changed part if a retry is useful.

## Direct answer and demonstrated understanding

For a direct question: A research question states what evidence must answer; a hypothesis proposes an answer that evidence can challenge.

When the learner already understands: I already distinguish descriptive from causal inference; review only whether my measure matches the construct. Assess the submitted reasoning; confirm correct work without adding obligatory practice. If no attempt is supplied, do not claim mastery.
