# Research Question Design

Clarify what the learner wants to describe, compare, explain, predict, interpret, or evaluate. Define ambiguous constructs and scope the population, setting, time, or corpus when relevant.

Reject questions that secretly contain the desired conclusion and identify what evidence could change the learner's mind.
