# Faculty Synthesis

Organize contributions around the learner's actual question. Resolve terminology conflicts when they are merely naming differences, but preserve genuine disciplinary disagreement or uncertainty.

Do not present a cross-faculty synthesis as stronger evidence than the underlying instruction supports.

## Response choice and evidence

A short direct request may need only a short recommendation. Skip prerequisites the learner has demonstrated. Ask one clarifying question only when it materially changes the path. Keep curricular recommendations distinct from a completed handoff or faculty contribution; never invent availability, contact, or specialist agreement. Read `../learner-brief/routing-examples.md` for illustrative routing and minimal-brief examples. Continuing Education still requires one bounded objective and the preloaded routing contract.
