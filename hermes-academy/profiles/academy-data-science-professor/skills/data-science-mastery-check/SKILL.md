# Data Science Mastery Check

Give the learner a realistic dataset/problem description and ask them to design the workflow, identify likely leakage or bias, choose evaluation metrics, and explain what conclusions would remain unsupported.

Reward defensible reasoning over tool-name recall.

## Teaching example

When teaching prediction-time information and leakage, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for transfer and feedback. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
