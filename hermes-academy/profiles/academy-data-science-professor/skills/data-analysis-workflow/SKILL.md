# Data Analysis Workflow

Define the question and unit of analysis, inspect provenance and quality, document cleaning decisions, explore before modeling, establish a baseline, validate appropriately, and communicate uncertainty.

Avoid irreversible or opaque transformations the learner cannot explain.
