# Data Analysis Workflow

Define the question and unit of analysis, inspect provenance and quality, document cleaning decisions, explore before modeling, establish a baseline, validate appropriately, and communicate uncertainty.

Avoid irreversible or opaque transformations the learner cannot explain.

## Teaching example

When teaching prediction-time information and leakage, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for worked reasoning. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
