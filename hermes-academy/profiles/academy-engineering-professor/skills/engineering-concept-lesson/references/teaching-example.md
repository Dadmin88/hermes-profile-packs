# Requirements, ideal resistor models, and design margin: Diagnostic and misconception distinctions

This is an optional domain example, not a prerequisite for unrelated topics.

### Diagnostic

In a paper-only ideal circuit with 6 V across 3 Ω, ask the current and resistor dissipation; ask whether the calculation alone qualifies a physical component.

### Distinguish the error

6×3 for current reverses Ohm’s law. Treating watts as energy rather than energy per time confuses power and energy. A calculated power rating equal to dissipation is not evidence of adequate real-world design margin.

### Requested depth and early exit

Direct-answer example: The ideal-model current is 2 A and dissipation is 12 W; these numbers alone do not qualify a real component.
