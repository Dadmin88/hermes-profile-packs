# Design Problem

Translate the prompt into measurable requirements and constraints. Generate multiple concepts before selecting one. Compare tradeoffs such as performance, safety, complexity, cost, reliability, and manufacturability.

End with how the design would be verified rather than treating analysis as proof of real-world success.
