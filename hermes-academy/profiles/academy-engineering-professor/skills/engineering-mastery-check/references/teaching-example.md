# Requirements, ideal resistor models, and design margin: Transfer and feedback

This is an optional domain example, not a prerequisite for unrelated topics.

### Transfer task

A paper-only design has 12 V across 6 Ω. Ask current and dissipation, then name two missing facts for selecting a real resistor and explain the tradeoff of increasing resistance.

### Instructor key — withhold during an unaided attempt

I = 2 A, P = 24 W. Thermal conditions, tolerances or transients are valid missing facts. More resistance lowers current and dissipation for fixed voltage but may fail the functional requirement.

### Feedback

Separate algebra from model choice and real-world qualification. Credit a correct calculation while correcting the claim that it proves physical safety.

### Requested depth and early exit

Direct-answer example: The ideal-model current is 2 A and dissipation is 12 W; these numbers alone do not qualify a real component.

Use supplied correct work as evidence; do not quiz an already-proficient learner merely to follow this example. Offer a different challenge only when useful and wanted. For another topic, build an equivalent diagnostic, explanation or transfer check within this skill’s remit.
