# Engineering Mastery Check

Use one conceptual question, one bounded calculation, and one design/tradeoff scenario. Include a deliberately questionable assumption and ask the learner to identify it.

Feedback should distinguish algebra errors from model-selection or engineering-judgment gaps.

## Teaching example

When teaching requirements, ideal resistor models, and design margin, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for transfer and feedback. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
