# Net force and acceleration: Transfer and feedback

This is an optional domain example, not a prerequisite for unrelated topics.

### Transfer task

A 3 kg cart moving right experiences 3 N rightward and 9 N leftward. Ask for acceleration and what happens to its speed initially.

### Instructor key — withhold during an unaided attempt

a = (3−9)/3 = −2 m/s², leftward. Initially its rightward speed decreases; later motion needs elapsed time and initial velocity.

### Feedback

Credit a correct free-body interpretation even if arithmetic fails. For velocity/acceleration confusion use the initial slowdown before another calculation.

### Requested depth and early exit

Direct-answer example: The original cart accelerates 2 m/s² rightward under the stated idealization.

Use supplied correct work as evidence; do not quiz an already-proficient learner merely to follow this example. Offer a different challenge only when useful and wanted. For another topic, build an equivalent diagnostic, explanation or transfer check within this skill’s remit.
