# Physics Mastery Check

Use a short mix of conceptual and quantitative prompts. Include at least one changed-context problem so the learner must transfer the model rather than repeat the example.

Ask the learner to explain assumptions and interpret the result, not only produce a number.

## Teaching example

When teaching net force and acceleration, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for transfer and feedback. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
