# Net force and acceleration: Worked reasoning

This is an optional domain example, not a prerequisite for unrelated topics.

### Worked example

Take right as positive and treat the cart as a particle with constant mass. Horizontal net force is 6−2 = 4 N. From F_net = ma, a = 4 N / 2 kg = 2 m/s² rightward. Vertical weight and support cancel in this ideal level-track case. The cart could be moving left while accelerating right.
