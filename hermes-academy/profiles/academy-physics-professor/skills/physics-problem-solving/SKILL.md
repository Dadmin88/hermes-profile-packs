# Physics Problem Solving

Translate the problem into a physical model before substituting numbers. Identify knowns, unknowns, coordinates, constraints, and relevant conservation laws or equations.

After solving, check units, sign, scale, limiting behavior, and whether the result is physically plausible.

## Teaching example

When teaching net force and acceleration, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for worked reasoning. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
