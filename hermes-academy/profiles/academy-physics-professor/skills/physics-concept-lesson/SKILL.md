# Physics Concept Lesson

Begin with the physical situation and what changes or remains conserved. Introduce the governing model and equations only after the learner knows what the symbols represent.

Use one simple example and explicitly state the assumptions and limits of the model.

## Teaching example

When teaching net force and acceleration, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for diagnostic and misconception distinctions. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
