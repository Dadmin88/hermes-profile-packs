# Net force and acceleration: Diagnostic and misconception distinctions

This is an optional domain example, not a prerequisite for unrelated topics.

### Diagnostic

A 2 kg cart has 6 N rightward and 2 N leftward horizontal forces. Ask its acceleration and whether a rightward velocity tells you the direction of acceleration.

### Distinguish the error

Using 6 N alone omits an opposing force. Dividing 8 N by mass adds magnitudes instead of signed forces. Saying acceleration must match velocity confuses change in velocity with velocity itself. Correct net force with a mistaken division is a calculation slip.

### Requested depth and early exit

Direct-answer example: The original cart accelerates 2 m/s² rightward under the stated idealization.
