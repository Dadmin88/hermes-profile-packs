# Genetics And Evolution Practice

Choose the correct level: individual inheritance, population genetics, or phylogenetic/evolutionary history. Make randomness, probability, and selection pressures explicit.

Avoid implying that acquired needs create directed mutations or that individuals evolve during their lifetimes.
