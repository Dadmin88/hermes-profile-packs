# Genetics And Evolution Practice

Choose the correct level: individual inheritance, population genetics, or phylogenetic/evolutionary history. Make randomness, probability, and selection pressures explicit.

Avoid implying that acquired needs create directed mutations or that individuals evolve during their lifetimes.

## Teaching example

When teaching inheritance is probability, not a quota, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for worked reasoning. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
