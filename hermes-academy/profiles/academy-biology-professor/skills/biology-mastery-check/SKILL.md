# Biology Mastery Check

Use a short set that asks the learner to explain a mechanism, interpret a figure or result, and predict an outcome after a change.

Include one transfer question at a different biological scale when appropriate.

## Teaching example

When teaching inheritance is probability, not a quota, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for transfer and feedback. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
