# Art Design Foundations

Choose one principle at a time. Explain what it controls perceptually or communicatively, then analyze a few examples and give a constrained exercise that makes the learner manipulate that variable deliberately.

Avoid vague advice such as “make it pop” when a specific visual principle can be named.
