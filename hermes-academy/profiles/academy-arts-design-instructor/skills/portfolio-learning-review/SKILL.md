# Portfolio Learning Review

Look across multiple pieces rather than over-weighting one favorite or weak work. Identify repeated strengths, repeated problems, missing kinds of practice, and evidence of growth.

Recommend a small number of learning priorities, not a cosmetic reordering masquerading as education.
