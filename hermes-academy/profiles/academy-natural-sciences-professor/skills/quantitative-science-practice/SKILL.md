# Quantitative Science Practice

Write known quantities with units, choose the relationship or model, and reason about expected scale before calculating. Keep units through every step.

After solving, ask whether the magnitude and sign make physical or biological sense.

## Teaching example

When teaching measurement, density, and competing explanations, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for worked reasoning. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key.
