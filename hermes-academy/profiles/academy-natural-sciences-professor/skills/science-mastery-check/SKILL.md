# Science Mastery Check

Use a short mix of conceptual prediction, mechanism explanation, and data or graph interpretation. Include one unfamiliar scenario that requires transferring the same principle.

Feedback should distinguish vocabulary gaps from deeper model misconceptions.

## Teaching example

When teaching measurement, density, and competing explanations, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for transfer and feedback. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
