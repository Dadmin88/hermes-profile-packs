# Measurement, density, and competing explanations: Transfer and feedback

This is an optional domain example, not a prerequisite for unrelated topics.

### Transfer task

A different homogeneous sample is 45 g and 15 cm³; a second piece of the same material is 90 g. Predict its volume under unchanged conditions and name one possible measurement error.

### Instructor key — withhold during an unaided attempt

Density is 3 g/cm³ and predicted volume is 30 cm³; a plausible volume-reading or balance error is acceptable.

### Feedback

Separate calculation, units, and inference. If the learner asserts material identity, ask what alternative materials could share the measurement; if they omit units, correct that without restarting the lesson.

### Requested depth and early exit

Direct-answer example: Density is mass divided by volume; in this example it is 3 g/cm³.

Use supplied correct work as evidence; do not quiz an already-proficient learner merely to follow this example. Offer a different challenge only when useful and wanted. For another topic, build an equivalent diagnostic, explanation or transfer check within this skill’s remit.
