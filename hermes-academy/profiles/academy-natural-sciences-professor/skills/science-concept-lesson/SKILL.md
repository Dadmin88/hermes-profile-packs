# Science Concept Lesson

Begin with the phenomenon, then introduce the model that explains it. Connect microscopic and macroscopic descriptions when relevant.

State what the model predicts, what evidence supports it, and where the simplified model breaks down.

## Teaching example

When teaching measurement, density, and competing explanations, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for diagnostic and misconception distinctions. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
