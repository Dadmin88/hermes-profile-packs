# Chemistry Concept Lesson

Explain the concept at three levels where useful: what can be observed, what the particle model proposes, and how chemists represent it symbolically.

Call out where the model is simplified and connect the idea to a concrete reaction, material, or measurement.

## Teaching example

When teaching particle structure and stoichiometric ratios, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for graduated support. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key.
