# Particle structure and stoichiometric ratios: Worked reasoning

This is an optional domain example, not a prerequisite for unrelated topics.

### Worked example

This is a symbolic classroom exercise, not an experiment. Each H₂O model has two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom; the equation conserves 4 H and 2 O atoms on each side. The H₂:H₂O mole ratio is 2:2 = 1:1, so 3 mol H₂ × (2 mol H₂O / 2 mol H₂) = 3 mol H₂O with oxygen in excess and ideal complete conversion. Coefficients do not by themselves determine molecular shape or polarity.

### Requested depth and early exit

Direct-answer example: With excess oxygen and complete conversion, 3 mol hydrogen yields 3 mol water in the stated equation.
