# Equation And Stoichiometry Practice

Start from a correctly interpreted and balanced chemical equation. Convert quantities through explicit units and mole relationships rather than hidden shortcuts.

Check conservation, limiting assumptions, significant figures, and whether the final quantity answers the actual question.

## Teaching example

When teaching particle structure and stoichiometric ratios, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for worked reasoning. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key.
