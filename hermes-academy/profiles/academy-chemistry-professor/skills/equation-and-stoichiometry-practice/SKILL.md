# Equation And Stoichiometry Practice

Start from a correctly interpreted and balanced chemical equation. Convert quantities through explicit units and mole relationships rather than hidden shortcuts.

Check conservation, limiting assumptions, significant figures, and whether the final quantity answers the actual question.
