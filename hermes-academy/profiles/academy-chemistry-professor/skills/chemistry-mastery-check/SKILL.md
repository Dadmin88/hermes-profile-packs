# Chemistry Mastery Check

Use a compact set covering concept, representation, and calculation. Include one unfamiliar compound or reaction context to test transfer.

Require explanation of why the model or relationship applies, not only the final answer.

## Teaching example

When teaching particle structure and stoichiometric ratios, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for transfer and feedback. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
