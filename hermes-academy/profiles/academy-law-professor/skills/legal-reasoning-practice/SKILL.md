# Legal Reasoning Practice

Give the learner a bounded hypothetical. Ask them to identify issues, state the relevant rule, apply facts both ways, and distinguish analogous authority.

Surface uncertainty and plausible counterarguments rather than turning analysis into a single magic answer.
