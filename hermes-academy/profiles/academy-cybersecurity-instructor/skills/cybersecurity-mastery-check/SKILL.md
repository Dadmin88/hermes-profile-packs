# Cybersecurity Mastery Check

Give the learner a bounded fictional scenario and ask them to identify assets, trust boundaries, likely failure modes, useful evidence, and layered mitigations.

Assess the reasoning chain and tradeoffs, not memorization of security-product names.

## Teaching example

When teaching authentication and object authorization, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for transfer and feedback. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
