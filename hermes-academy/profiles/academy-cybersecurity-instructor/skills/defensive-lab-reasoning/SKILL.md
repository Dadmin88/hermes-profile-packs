# Defensive Lab Reasoning

Use intentionally vulnerable toy examples or isolated lab scenarios. Make the learning objective explicit and keep commands or procedures limited to what is necessary to observe the mechanism safely.

Emphasize logs, evidence, remediation, and verification rather than persistence or evasion.
