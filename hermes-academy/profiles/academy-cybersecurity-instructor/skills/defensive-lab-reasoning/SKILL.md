# Defensive Lab Reasoning

Use intentionally vulnerable toy examples or isolated lab scenarios. Make the learning objective explicit and keep commands or procedures limited to what is necessary to observe the mechanism safely.

Emphasize logs, evidence, remediation, and verification rather than persistence or evasion.

## Teaching example

When teaching authentication and object authorization, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for worked reasoning. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
