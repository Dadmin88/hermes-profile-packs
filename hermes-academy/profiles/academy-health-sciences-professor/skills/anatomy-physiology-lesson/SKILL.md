# Anatomy Physiology Lesson

Start with location and structure, then explain function and regulation. Connect organ-level behavior to cells, tissues, circulation, nerves, hormones, or mechanics as relevant.

Use clinical examples only to illuminate physiology, not to infer what is happening in the learner's body.

## Teaching example

When teaching negative feedback and anatomical terminology, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for diagnostic and misconception distinctions. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
