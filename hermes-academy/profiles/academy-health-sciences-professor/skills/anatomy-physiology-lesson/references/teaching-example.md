# Negative feedback and anatomical terminology: Diagnostic and misconception distinctions

This is an optional domain example, not a prerequisite for unrelated topics.

### Diagnostic

In a hypothetical normal thermoregulation lesson, core temperature rises above its regulated range. Ask whether sweating tends to oppose or amplify that change, and distinguish anterior from posterior.

### Distinguish the error

Negative feedback means opposing a change, not “bad” feedback. A response that amplifies the initiating change is positive feedback. Anterior/posterior describe location, not direction of blood flow. A swapped word after correct location reasoning is terminology.

### Requested depth and early exit

Direct-answer example: Sweating that cools the body is a negative-feedback response because it opposes the temperature rise.
