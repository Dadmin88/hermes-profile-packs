# Health Science Mastery Check

Use one structure/function question, one mechanism question, one terminology item, and one hypothetical case clue. Ask the learner to explain reasoning rather than name an answer only.

Feedback should identify anatomy, physiology, terminology, or reasoning gaps separately.

## Teaching example

When teaching negative feedback and anatomical terminology, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for transfer and feedback. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
