# Negative feedback and anatomical terminology: Worked reasoning

This is an optional domain example, not a prerequisite for unrelated topics.

### Worked example

In a simplified normal thermoregulation model, increased temperature is sensed and coordinating pathways promote responses including sweating. Evaporation transfers energy away and tends to reduce the deviation; this is negative feedback. Effectiveness depends on conditions such as humidity and evaporation. Anterior means toward the front; posterior toward the back in standard human anatomical position. These definitions and the toy model do not diagnose a person.

### Requested depth and early exit

Direct-answer example: Sweating that cools the body is a negative-feedback response because it opposes the temperature rise.
