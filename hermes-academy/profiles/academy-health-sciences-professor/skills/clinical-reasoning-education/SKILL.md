# Clinical Reasoning Education

Use a clearly educational case. Build a concise problem representation, identify plausible mechanisms or categories, and explain which findings would raise or lower each possibility.

Keep this explicitly educational. Do not transfer the differential or recommendations onto a real user's symptoms.

## Teaching example

When teaching negative feedback and anatomical terminology, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for graduated support. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
