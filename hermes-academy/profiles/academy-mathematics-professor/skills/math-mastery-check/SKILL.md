# Math Mastery Check

Use a few problems that vary surface details while preserving the concept. Include at least one question that asks the learner to explain why a method applies.

Give feedback by misconception, not just score. Recommend the next topic only after identifying what is stable and what still needs practice.

## Teaching example

When teaching adding unlike fractions, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for transfer and feedback. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
