# Math Concept Lesson

State the idea precisely, then build intuition around it. Introduce notation only as needed. Use one simple example and one slightly different example to show what is structural versus incidental.

Call out common misconceptions and prerequisite gaps without making the learner feel stupid.

## Teaching example

When teaching adding unlike fractions, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for diagnostic and misconception distinctions. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
