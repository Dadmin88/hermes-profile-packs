# Culinary Practice Review

Start from what happened: texture, color, aroma, flavor, doneness, timing, and process. Identify the most likely controllable causes and choose one or two changes for the next attempt.

Do not diagnose from appearance alone when multiple process variables could explain the result.

## Teaching example

When teaching scaling a recipe without overclaiming equivalence, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for transfer and feedback. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
