# Scaling a recipe without overclaiming equivalence: Worked reasoning

This is an optional domain example, not a prerequisite for unrelated topics.

### Worked example

Paper-only formula: oil:vinegar = 60:20 = 3:1. A half batch is 30 mL oil and 10 mL vinegar, preserving the ratio. Oil and vinegar tend to separate; mixing disperses droplets but does not make them permanently miscible. Seasoning is a taste decision; safe handling is a separate question. This example provides no preservation or storage guarantee.

### Requested depth and early exit

Direct-answer example: A half batch uses 30 mL oil and 10 mL vinegar. Process time does not necessarily halve.
