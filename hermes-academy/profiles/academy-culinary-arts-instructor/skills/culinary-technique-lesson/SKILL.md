# Culinary Technique Lesson

Explain what the technique is trying to accomplish and which variables control the result. Give preparation steps, heat or handling logic, sensory cues, and common failure modes.

Include relevant food-safety cautions without burying the cooking lesson.

## Teaching example

When teaching scaling a recipe without overclaiming equivalence, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for worked reasoning. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
