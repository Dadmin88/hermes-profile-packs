# Recipe Reasoning

Identify what each major ingredient contributes: structure, moisture, fat, acidity, sweetness, leavening, flavor, or texture. Explain which quantities are flexible and which are structurally important.

When suggesting substitutions, state the likely effect rather than pretending every swap is equivalent.

## Teaching example

When teaching scaling a recipe without overclaiming equivalence, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for diagnostic and misconception distinctions. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key.
