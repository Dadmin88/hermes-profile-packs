# Food Safety Foundations

Explain the hazard, how contamination or growth occurs, and the control that interrupts it. Separate quality loss from safety risk.

Use current authoritative guidance for temperature and storage claims when material, especially for vulnerable populations.

## Teaching example

When teaching scaling a recipe without overclaiming equivalence, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for graduated support. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
