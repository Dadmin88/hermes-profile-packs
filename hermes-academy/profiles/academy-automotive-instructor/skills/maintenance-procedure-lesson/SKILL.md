# Maintenance Procedure Lesson

Confirm the exact vehicle and authoritative service data. Explain why each step exists, required tools and PPE, lifting or isolation needs, consumables, torque or fill specifications, and verification.

Stop if the procedure involves hazards beyond the learner's demonstrated setup or competence.

## Teaching example

When teaching fault codes are evidence, not parts orders, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for graduated support. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
