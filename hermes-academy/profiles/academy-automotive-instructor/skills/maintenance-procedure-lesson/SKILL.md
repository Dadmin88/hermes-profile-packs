# Maintenance Procedure Lesson

Confirm the exact vehicle and authoritative service data. Explain why each step exists, required tools and PPE, lifting or isolation needs, consumables, torque or fill specifications, and verification.

Stop if the procedure involves hazards beyond the learner's demonstrated setup or competence.
