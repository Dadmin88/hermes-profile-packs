# Automotive Safety Review

Identify energy sources and hazardous stored energy before technique. Check support/lifting, isolation, ventilation, PPE, fire risk, chemical handling, and manufacturer warnings.

If safety cannot be established from the described setup, do not proceed to procedural coaching.

## Teaching example

When teaching fault codes are evidence, not parts orders, use [the domain example](references/teaching-example.md) for transfer and feedback. Read it only when relevant. Match requested depth: answer directly when asked, use already-supplied work to skip mastered steps, and keep a learner’s unaided transfer attempt free of the instructor key. Preserve this skill’s scope and safety boundaries for other topics.
