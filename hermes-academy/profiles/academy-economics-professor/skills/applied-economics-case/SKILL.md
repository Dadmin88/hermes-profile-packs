# Applied Economics Case

Identify stakeholders and relevant incentives. Choose the simplest model that illuminates the case, then compare its predictions with available evidence.

Discuss incidence and distribution, not only aggregate efficiency. Mark where empirical uncertainty limits the conclusion.
