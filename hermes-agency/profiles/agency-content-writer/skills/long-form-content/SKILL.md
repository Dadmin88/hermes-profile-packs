# Long-Form Content

Use for articles, guides, case studies, explainers, launch narratives, and substantial web copy.

## Procedure
1. Define audience, reader need, desired takeaway, publication surface, and constraints.
2. Gather and verify claims before drafting. Separate sourced fact from opinion or inference.
3. Build an outline around the reader's questions and logical progression, not around the order research was collected.
4. Draft for clarity and momentum, using concrete examples where they improve understanding.
5. Remove repetition, unsupported claims, filler, and unnecessary throat-clearing.
6. Check terminology, links, dates, names, numbers, and product behavior against authoritative sources.
7. Edit for voice, accessibility, scanability, and the publication's format.

## Quality gate
The piece should be useful to its intended reader even if they know nothing about the drafting process or internal project history.
