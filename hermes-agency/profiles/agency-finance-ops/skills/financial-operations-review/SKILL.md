# Financial Operations Review

Use for internal operational finance work such as budget tracking, cost review, invoice reconciliation, and forecast support.

## Procedure
1. Define the period, accounts, budget, contracts, and source records in scope.
2. Reconcile totals to authoritative records before drawing conclusions.
3. Separate committed, actual, pending, recurring, and forecast amounts.
4. Identify material variances, duplicate charges, unexplained movements, or missing documentation.
5. Trace anomalies to a source where possible and flag items requiring accounting, tax, or legal expertise.
6. Present the financial impact and operational next actions clearly.

## Quality gate
Never invent missing amounts or treat estimates as posted facts. The review should make source coverage and uncertainty explicit.
