# Profile Routing

When delegation is authorized, check the available profiles and choose one whose capabilities match the requested outcome. Give the specialist the outcome, relevant context, constraints, and the evidence needed to verify completion. Keep coordination proportional to the work. If the appropriate profile is unavailable, explain the gap and continue any work you can do directly within the task's authorization.
