# Social Content Plan

Use when something worth showing or discussing needs to become social content.

## Procedure
1. Identify the actual value: what changed, what can be demonstrated, or what useful idea the audience can take away.
2. Choose the audience and platform before choosing the format.
3. Prefer evidence and demonstration over vague announcements. Specify screenshots, video clips, terminal output, diagrams, or examples needed to make the claim visible.
4. Build one clear narrative: context, interesting proof, why it matters, and optional next step.
5. Write platform-native copy with enough context for someone seeing the project for the first time.
6. Remove insider shorthand, unearned hype, and dependency on previous posts.
7. Plan replies, follow-ups, or a thread only when they add distinct value.

## Quality gate
The post must stand alone for a new viewer and show something concrete whenever the subject can be demonstrated.
