# Brand Application Review

Use when a website, campaign, product surface, document, social asset, or physical application needs brand-quality approval.

## Procedure
1. Identify the artifact's audience, channel, objective, format constraints, and applicable brand standards.
2. Review identity elements such as logo, typography, color, imagery, iconography, layout, and motion for correct use and relationships.
3. Evaluate whether the application communicates the intended message hierarchy before checking decorative fidelity.
4. Check tone and visual character against the brand while allowing channel-appropriate adaptation.
5. Inspect edge contexts such as small formats, long copy, localization, dark backgrounds, accessibility, print production, or responsive layouts as relevant.
6. Separate true brand inconsistency from a justified exception that supports the artifact's purpose.
7. Prioritize findings by recognition, clarity, trust, and system coherence rather than minor cosmetic variance.
8. Capture any recurring ambiguity as a candidate improvement to the brand system itself.

## Decision rules
- Brand consistency is coherent recognition, not identical layouts everywhere.
- Channel adaptation is allowed when the underlying identity principles remain intact.
- Do not block on tiny deviations that have no perceptual or strategic consequence.
- Repeated exceptions may reveal a missing system rule rather than bad execution.

## Quality gate
The application passes when the brand remains recognizable and strategically coherent, message hierarchy works for the channel, material identity violations are resolved, justified adaptations are intentional, and recurring ambiguity feeds back into the system.
