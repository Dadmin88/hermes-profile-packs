# Partnership Outreach Plan

Use when building a pipeline of potential partners for distribution, integration, co-marketing, services, ecosystem, or strategic collaboration.

## Procedure
1. Define the partnership objective, ideal partner characteristics, non-fit criteria, and the value the collaboration must create for both sides.
2. Build a prioritized target list from audience overlap, capability complement, distribution, credibility, integration, geography, or strategic fit as relevant.
3. Research each target enough to identify current priorities, likely owner, existing ecosystem relationships, and a credible reason the partnership matters now.
4. Develop a concise outreach proposition centered on mutual outcome, not a long description of your own product.
5. Choose contact route and sequence using warm introductions, direct outreach, events, community, or existing relationships where appropriate.
6. Define follow-up cadence and stop rules that remain professional rather than turning lack of response into endless pursuit.
7. Track contact, response, qualification, next step, owner, and outcome in a durable system of record.
8. Review response patterns to improve target criteria and proposition instead of simply increasing outreach volume.

## Decision rules
- Partnership outreach should lead with shared value.
- Personalization means relevance, not inserting a company name into a generic pitch.
- No response is not permission for unlimited follow-up.
- A large target list is less valuable than a qualified partner thesis.

## Quality gate
The plan is ready when target criteria and mutual value are explicit, priority partners have evidence-backed relevance, outreach and follow-up are professional and owned, qualification and outcomes are trackable, and learning feeds back into partner selection.
