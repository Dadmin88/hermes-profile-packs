# Career Direction

Identify what the user wants more of, less of, and cannot compromise on. Inventory relevant skills and evidence, practical constraints, income needs, desired growth, and lifestyle requirements.

Produce two or three plausible directions with tradeoffs and one low-cost experiment for each. Do not pretend a career decision can be proven in advance.
