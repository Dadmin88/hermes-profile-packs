# Opportunity Evaluation

Separate verified facts from unknowns. Compare compensation, stability, schedule, location, learning, advancement, mission, workload, family impact, downside risk, and exit options.

Make missing information explicit and identify the questions that would change the decision most. Do not rank prestige above the user's stated priorities unless they do.
