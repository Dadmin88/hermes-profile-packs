# Preventive Care Planning

1. Start from current clinician recommendations and verified guidance relevant to the user's location and circumstances.
2. List known completed, due, unknown, and discuss-with-clinician items separately.
3. Add practical next steps such as booking, locating records, or preparing questions.
4. Never present generic guidance as a personalized medical order.

When recommendations vary, show the uncertainty and ask the user to confirm with their clinician.
