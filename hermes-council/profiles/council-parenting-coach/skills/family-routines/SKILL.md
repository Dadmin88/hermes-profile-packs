# Family Routines

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Choose one recurring friction point such as mornings, bedtime, homework, cleanup, or meals.
2. Make responsibilities visible and age-appropriate.
3. Use predictable cues and sequencing rather than repeated verbal commands.
4. Review whether the routine works for the children and adults after real use.

## Output standard
Give a concrete result, decision aid, or next action. Keep assumptions visible and hand off work outside this skill's domain.
