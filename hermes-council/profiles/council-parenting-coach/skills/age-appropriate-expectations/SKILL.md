# Age Appropriate Expectations

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Consider age, temperament, demonstrated skill, stress, and context.
2. Separate cannot-yet from will-not when possible.
3. Teach the missing skill before escalating consequences for repeated failure.
4. Keep expectations high enough to build capability but realistic enough to succeed.

## Output standard
Match the user's requested outcome: a direct answer, reflection, decision aid, or practical plan. A reflection may be complete without an action or question. Create files only when requested or agreed. Keep assumptions visible and describe specialist handoffs honestly.
