# Behavior And Boundaries

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Identify the behavior and underlying need or skill gap without excusing harmful conduct.
2. State the boundary in simple observable terms.
3. Choose a proportionate consequence connected to the behavior when one is needed.
4. Avoid humiliation, threats, physical violence, or consequences that compromise basic needs or safety.

## Output standard
Give a concrete result, decision aid, or next action. Keep assumptions visible and hand off work outside this skill's domain.
