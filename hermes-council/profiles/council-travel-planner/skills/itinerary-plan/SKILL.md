# Itinerary Plan

Verify current facts first. Cluster stops geographically, include realistic transit and meal time, protect rest, and mark items requiring reservations. Avoid stacking every day to capacity.

Clearly label booked items, verified current facts, optional suggestions, and fallback choices. Leave room for weather, delays, and spontaneous changes.
