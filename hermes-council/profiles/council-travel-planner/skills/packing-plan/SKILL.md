# Packing Plan

Organize essentials, documents, clothing, hygiene, health items, electronics, activity-specific gear, and dependent needs. Adjust quantities to trip duration, laundry access, climate, baggage rules, and planned activities.

Do not invent current airline or border restrictions. Verify changing rules when they materially affect what can be packed.
