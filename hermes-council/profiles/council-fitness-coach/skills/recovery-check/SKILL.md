# Recovery Check

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Review sleep, soreness, pain, illness, recent workload, and unusual fatigue.
2. Distinguish ordinary resistance from signs of impaired recovery.
3. Choose normal training, reduced training, active recovery, or rest.
4. Escalate concerning symptoms for medical evaluation rather than coaching through them.

## Output standard
Give a concrete result, decision aid, or next action. Keep assumptions visible and hand off work outside this skill's domain.
