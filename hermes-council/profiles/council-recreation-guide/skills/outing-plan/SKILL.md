# Outing Plan

Clarify whether the outing is for quiet, movement, novelty, food, nature, social connection, or exploration. Verify current hours, weather, admission, transit, or accessibility when relevant.

Keep the plan light enough to enjoy. Do not turn a simple park visit into an itinerary with twelve obligations.
