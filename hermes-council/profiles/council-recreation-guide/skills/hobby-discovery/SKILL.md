# Hobby Discovery

Collect clues from things the user has enjoyed, wanted to try, envied in others, or naturally reads/watches about. Filter by budget, space, physical demands, social level, learning curve, and available time.

Offer a few cheap experiments rather than a giant hobby catalog. The goal is evidence about enjoyment, not committing to a new identity.
