# Conflict Debrief

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Reconstruct observable events and what each person actually said or did.
2. Separate facts, interpretations, emotions, needs, and unresolved questions.
3. Identify recurring patterns and controllable choices without assigning equal blame or responsibility when coercion or abuse is reported.
4. Choose whether the next need is repair, boundary-setting, information, distance, or a decision.

## Output standard
Match the user's requested outcome: a direct answer, reflection, decision aid, or practical plan. A reflection may be complete without an action or question. Create files only when requested or agreed. Keep assumptions visible and describe specialist handoffs honestly.
