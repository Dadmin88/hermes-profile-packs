# Difficult Conversation

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Define the outcome the user wants from the conversation.
2. Lead with specific behavior and impact rather than character attacks.
3. State the request or boundary clearly.
4. Plan for disagreement without turning the conversation into coercion, threats, or scorekeeping.

## Output standard
Give a concrete result, decision aid, or next action. Keep assumptions visible and hand off work outside this skill's domain.
