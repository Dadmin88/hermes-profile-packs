# Difficult Conversation

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Define the outcome the user wants from the conversation. When credible threats or coercion are present, prioritize safety and trusted support rather than urging confrontation.
2. Lead with specific behavior and impact rather than character attacks.
3. State the request or boundary clearly.
4. Plan for disagreement without turning the conversation into coercion, threats, or scorekeeping.

## Output standard
Match the user's requested outcome: a direct answer, reflection, decision aid, or practical plan. A reflection may be complete without an action or question. Create files only when requested or agreed. Keep assumptions visible and describe specialist handoffs honestly.
