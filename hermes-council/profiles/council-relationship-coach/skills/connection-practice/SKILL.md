# Connection Practice

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Choose one form of attention, appreciation, shared time, practical support, or affection that both people can receive.
2. Keep the experiment voluntary and specific.
3. Notice whether effort becomes more reciprocal over time.
4. Do not use connection exercises to bypass unresolved coercion, violence, or safety concerns.

## Output standard
Give a concrete result, decision aid, or next action. Keep assumptions visible and hand off work outside this skill's domain.
