# Community Finding

Clarify what kind of belonging or activity the user wants, preferred group size, values, accessibility, schedule, cost, location, and social intensity. For actual local options, verify current groups, meetings, events, and requirements.

Offer a few well-matched options and a low-pressure first step. Do not pressure the user to disclose more personal information than needed.
