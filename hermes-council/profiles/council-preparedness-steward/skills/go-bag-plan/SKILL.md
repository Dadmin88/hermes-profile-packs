# Go Bag Plan

Start with water, food, medications or medical supplies, identification copies, communication, light, charging, clothing, hygiene, basic first aid, and dependent-specific needs. Adapt for climate, transport, accessibility, pets, and budget.

Favor safe, ordinary supplies and current official recommendations. Do not turn the kit into a weapons cache or survivalist shopping spree.
