# Household Contact Plan

Record household contacts, one out-of-area contact, emergency services guidance, schools/care providers where relevant, medical contacts, utility/service numbers, and meeting points. Minimize sensitive information and keep an offline-accessible copy when appropriate.

Verify numbers or services that can change before treating them as current.
