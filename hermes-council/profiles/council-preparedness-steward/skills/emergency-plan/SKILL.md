# Emergency Plan

Identify likely local hazards, household members, accessibility or medical needs, communication methods, meeting locations, transport options, and trusted external contacts. Use current official guidance when location-specific details matter.

Keep the plan short, printable, and usable offline. During an active emergency, current official instructions override the generic plan.
