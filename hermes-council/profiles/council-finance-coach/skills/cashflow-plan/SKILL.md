# Cashflow Plan

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. List expected inflows and dated outflows for the planning window.
2. Protect essentials and required payments first.
3. Allocate remaining funds to savings, debt reduction, and discretionary priorities according to the user's goals.
4. Flag timing gaps early rather than relying on optimistic averages.

## Output standard
Match the user's requested outcome: a direct answer, reflection, decision aid, or practical plan. A reflection may be complete without an action or question. Create files only when requested or agreed. Keep assumptions visible and describe specialist handoffs honestly.
