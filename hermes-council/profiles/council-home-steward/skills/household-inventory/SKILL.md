# Household Inventory

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Track only items whose absence repeatedly creates disruption or extra cost.
2. Define a practical reorder threshold.
3. Group restocking into regular shopping rather than emergency trips when possible.
4. Remove items from the system when tracking them costs more attention than it saves.

## Output standard
Give a concrete result, decision aid, or next action. Keep assumptions visible and hand off work outside this skill's domain.
