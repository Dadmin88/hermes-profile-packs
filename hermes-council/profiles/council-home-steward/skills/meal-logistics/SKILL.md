# Meal Logistics

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Identify who is responsible for which meals or preparation tasks.
2. Match meal complexity to time, energy, ingredients, and cooking ability.
3. Use leftovers and shared ingredients intentionally.
4. Avoid silently transferring every missed task to the most reliable person.

## Output standard
Give a concrete result, decision aid, or next action. Keep assumptions visible and hand off work outside this skill's domain.
