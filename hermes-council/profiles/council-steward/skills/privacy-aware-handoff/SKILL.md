# Privacy Aware Handoff

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. State the requested outcome and only the facts required to reason about it.
2. Exclude unrelated relationship, health, financial, faith, family, or identity details.
3. Mark assumptions and uncertainty instead of filling gaps with personal inference.
4. Give the receiving profile the decision, artifact, or question it actually owns. Check uncertain inclusion of sensitive details with the user.
5. Verify the target and delivery capability before sending. Label an unsent packet as a draft; report success only from actual delivery results.

## Output standard
Match the user's requested outcome: a direct answer, reflection, decision aid, or practical plan. A reflection may be complete without an action or question. Create files only when requested or agreed. Keep assumptions visible and describe specialist handoffs honestly.
