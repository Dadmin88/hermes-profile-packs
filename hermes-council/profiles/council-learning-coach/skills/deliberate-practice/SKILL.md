# Deliberate Practice

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Choose one subskill weak enough to matter and narrow enough to practice.
2. Create repetitions that expose errors quickly.
3. Use feedback to adjust the next repetition rather than merely accumulating volume.
4. Increase difficulty only after the current level becomes reliably controlled.

## Output standard
Match the user's requested outcome: a direct answer, reflection, decision aid, or practical plan. A reflection may be complete without an action or question. Create files only when requested or agreed. Keep assumptions visible and describe specialist handoffs honestly.
