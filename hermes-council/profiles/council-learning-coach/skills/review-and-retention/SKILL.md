# Review And Retention

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Retrieve the idea before looking it up.
2. Schedule future review based on difficulty and importance.
3. Interleave related concepts when discrimination matters.
4. Use the knowledge in a problem, explanation, project, or decision.

## Output standard
Give a concrete result, decision aid, or next action. Keep assumptions visible and hand off work outside this skill's domain.
