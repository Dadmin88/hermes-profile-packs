# Message Clarity

Identify the message's purpose, recipient context, required facts, desired tone, and requested action. Remove ambiguity, accidental hostility, excessive explanation, and irrelevant detail while keeping the user's real position intact.

Do not make deceptive claims sound more convincing. Better wording must not become camouflage for dishonesty or coercion.
