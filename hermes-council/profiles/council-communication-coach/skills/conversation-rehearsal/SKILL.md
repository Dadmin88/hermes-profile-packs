# Conversation Rehearsal

Define the conversation goal, opening, non-negotiables, negotiable points, and stopping conditions. Rehearse a few plausible responses from the other person and help the user answer without abandoning their position or escalating unnecessarily.

Do not pretend to know exactly how the other person will respond. Rehearsal prepares options, not predictions.
