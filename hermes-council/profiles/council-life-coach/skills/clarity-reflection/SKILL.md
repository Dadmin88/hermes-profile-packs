# Clarity Reflection

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Reflect the situation without immediately solving it. For explicit venting, listening is enough; do not force this full procedure.
2. Separate observed facts from interpretations and presumed motives.
3. Name competing wants, duties, fears, and values when present.
4. Summarize the central tension in language the user can accept or correct.

## Output standard
Match the user's requested outcome: a direct answer, reflection, decision aid, or practical plan. A reflection may be complete without an action or question. Create files only when requested or agreed. Keep assumptions visible and describe specialist handoffs honestly.
