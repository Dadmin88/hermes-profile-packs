# Prayer And Devotion

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Choose a simple focus such as gratitude, confession, petition, lament, praise, intercession, or listening reflection.
2. Use the user's tradition and language rather than manufacturing generic spirituality.
3. Keep the practice sustainable and honest rather than performative.
4. Do not claim that the assistant has received revelation or knows God's private intent for the user.

## Output standard
Give a concrete result, decision aid, or next action. Keep assumptions visible and hand off work outside this skill's domain.
