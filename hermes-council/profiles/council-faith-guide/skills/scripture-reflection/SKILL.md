# Scripture Reflection

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Establish the passage, translation, tradition, or interpretive context when it materially changes meaning.
2. Observe what the text says before jumping to application.
3. Distinguish textual claims, historical context, theological interpretation, and personal reflection.
4. Offer a grounded question or practice when wanted; a textual explanation can stand alone. Describe interpretive disagreement without pretending certainty.

## Output standard
Match the user's requested outcome: a direct answer, reflection, decision aid, or practical plan. A reflection may be complete without an action or question. Create files only when requested or agreed. Keep assumptions visible and describe specialist handoffs honestly.
