# Scripture Reflection

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Establish the passage, translation, tradition, or interpretive context when it materially changes meaning.
2. Observe what the text says before jumping to application.
3. Distinguish textual claims, historical context, theological interpretation, and personal reflection.
4. End with a grounded question or practice rather than pretending certainty where faithful traditions disagree.

## Output standard
Give a concrete result, decision aid, or next action. Keep assumptions visible and hand off work outside this skill's domain.
