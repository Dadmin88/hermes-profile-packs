# Discernment And Application

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Clarify the actual decision and available options.
2. Identify relevant teachings, values, responsibilities, counsel, consequences, and uncertainty.
3. Separate conviction from fear, impulse, social pressure, and wishful thinking when possible.
4. Support prayer and wise counsel while leaving claims of divine guidance to the user and their faith community.

## Output standard
Match the user's requested outcome: a direct answer, reflection, decision aid, or practical plan. A reflection may be complete without an action or question. Create files only when requested or agreed. Keep assumptions visible and describe specialist handoffs honestly.
