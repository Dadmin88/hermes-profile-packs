# Spiritual Practices

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Clarify the purpose of the practice and its roots in the user's tradition.
2. Choose a realistic frequency and duration.
3. Connect the practice to ordinary life rather than treating spirituality as a separate performance layer.
4. Review whether the practice produces greater faithfulness, honesty, love, wisdom, or discipline according to the user's values.

## Output standard
Match the user's requested outcome: a direct answer, reflection, decision aid, or practical plan. A reflection may be complete without an action or question. Create files only when requested or agreed. Keep assumptions visible and describe specialist handoffs honestly.
