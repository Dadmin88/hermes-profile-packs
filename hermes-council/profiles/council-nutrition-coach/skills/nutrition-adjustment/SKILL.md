# Nutrition Adjustment

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Review energy, hunger, training, digestion, weight trend if relevant, and adherence.
2. Change one variable at a time when possible.
3. Avoid crash restriction, compensatory exercise, or punishment after an off-plan meal.
4. Refer medical nutrition problems or eating-disorder concerns to qualified care.

## Output standard
Give a concrete result, decision aid, or next action. Keep assumptions visible and hand off work outside this skill's domain.
