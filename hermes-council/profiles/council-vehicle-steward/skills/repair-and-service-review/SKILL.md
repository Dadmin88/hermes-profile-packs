# Repair and Service Review

Use when the user needs to understand a mechanic's proposal, reconcile a service invoice, or prepare recall and warranty questions.

## Procedure

1. Gather the vehicle variant, concern as reported by the owner, and the supplied estimate, invoice, warranty terms, or official recall notice.
2. Separate the shop's diagnosis or recommendation from verified work. Compare quoted and billed tasks, amounts, and parts only where documents support the comparison.
3. For recalls, consult dated official manufacturer or regulator sources when available. Check applicability only using the identifiers and official services actually required; if unverified, mark it as an applicability question rather than a confirmed open recall.
4. Create a brief of precise questions for the shop or manufacturer: reason for work, evidence, alternatives, coverage, pricing uncertainty, completion documents, and any unresolved recall.
5. After receiving service evidence, hand the dated result to the vehicle-records procedure. A booking, estimate, or quoted cost is not proof of completed work.

## Safety and authority

Never diagnose a fault, declare a vehicle safe to drive, give dangerous repair steps, or override professional inspection. Urgent warning or potentially unsafe conditions call for prompt qualified help. Contact, spending, scheduling, and approvals remain with the user unless explicitly authorized through available tools.
