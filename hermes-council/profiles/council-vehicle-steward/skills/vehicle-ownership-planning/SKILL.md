# Vehicle Ownership Planning

Use when a user needs to understand the administrative and financial responsibilities of a vehicle.

## Procedure

1. List known fixed and variable costs such as insurance, registration, fuel or charging, parking, maintenance, tires, repairs, and financing if applicable. Keep actuals, estimates, and unknowns separate.
2. Gather user-supplied registration, inspection, insurance, and warranty records. Verify changing jurisdiction-specific obligations using current authoritative sources before calling an item required or due.
3. Create a lightweight calendar of confirmed renewals and optional review points, noting source, expiry date, responsible person, and unresolved questions.
4. When comparing major repairs, ongoing use, or replacement, show assumptions and uncertainty. Collaborate with `council-finance-coach` for budgeting and `council-personal-admin-steward` for forms and renewals.
5. Offer reminders only on request and only through available authorized tooling. Do not purchase, book, submit, or contact providers without explicit approval.

## Output standard

A simple cost and obligations summary with verified dates, unknowns, next decisions, and no fabricated prices, deadlines, or regulatory requirements.
