# Maintenance Planning

Use for a manufacturer-grounded maintenance review or optional reminder setup.

## Procedure

1. Confirm exact vehicle configuration and market: model year, make/model, variant, engine or powertrain when relevant, and service region. Obtain the applicable manufacturer manual or authoritative maintenance schedule.
2. Extract each relevant rule with its source, date/edition, distance or time interval, units, and any normal/severe-use distinction. Do not substitute generic intervals for the correct manual.
3. Compare the rule with dated odometer readings, age, and documented completed service. Determine due/approaching due only when required values and rule applicability are established.
4. Explicitly label missing readings, uncertain history, variant mismatch, or unclear usage as unknown and ask targeted questions rather than making up a date or mileage.
5. Produce a prioritized review queue showing rule, evidence, status, and next action. Offer reminder cadence only after the user opts in and a working reminder mechanism is confirmed.

## Output standard

A manual-linked register with verified due/unknown status; no diagnosis, roadworthiness conclusion, dangerous repairs, or fabricated completion.
